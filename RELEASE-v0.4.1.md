# 一刻 · Yike Focus v0.4.1

圆环报告去掉了浅色底圈和描边，短扇区也有圆润的两端。宽屏用同色引线标出任务和时长，窄屏在圆环下方显示标注。

The donut report has rounded ends even on short slices and a cleaner outline. On wide screens, color-matched leader lines connect each slice to its task name and duration. On narrow screens, the labels sit below the donut.
