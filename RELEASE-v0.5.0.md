# 一刻 · Yike Focus v0.5.0

## 本次更新

- 专注倒计时到点可选择轻提示或持续响铃。轻提示可接着正向计时或自动开始休息。持续闹钟提供「继续专注」和「开始休息」。延长的时间和原倒计时合成一条专注记录。
- 点当前任务即可快速切换。专注结束后显示已记录的任务和时长，支持补记和修改归属。
- 点报告圆环的扇区或标注，可以筛选对应的专注记录。
- 安卓通知显示任务与计时状态，并提供暂停和继续。

## What's new

- Choose a soft chime or a ringing alarm when your focus countdown ends. The chime can continue timing or start a break automatically. The alarm offers a choice. Overtime and the planned focus time are saved as one session.
- Tap the current task to switch quickly. A completion receipt shows what was logged. You can add or change the task afterward.
- Tap a report slice or label to filter session history.
- Pause or resume from the Android timer notification and see the current task at a glance.
